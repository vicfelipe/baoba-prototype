# Prompts de concept art

Um prompt por região para gerar pranchas de direção de arte, na ordem dos slices (Game Design, seção 18). Todos repetem o mesmo estilo e o mesmo layout: à esquerda, a cena principal; no alto à direita, personagens e criaturas em escala; embaixo à direita, paleta, sombreamento e padrões da região. Os prompts estão em inglês porque a maioria dos geradores responde melhor nesse idioma.

## Como usar

- Copie o bloco inteiro da região. Se o gerador se perder com tanta informação, gere os três blocos (cena, personagens e faixa de direção) separadamente e monte a prancha depois.
- As pranchas saem sem texto, porque os geradores erram letras; nomes e códigos de cor entram depois, num editor.
- Os nomes do GDD ficam fora dos prompts, na linha "Elementos do GDD", para o gerador não tentar escrever ou copiar nada.
- Antes de aprovar, passe o resultado pelo checklist da aba Guia de arte, seção 7, e pela revisão cultural.
- Use um gerador com licença comercial clara e sem restrição de território (aba Especificações técnicas, seção 15).

## 1. Mata Viva (slice 1, níveis 1 a 10)

**Elementos do GDD:** palafitas, Sumaúma-Mãe, Curupira corrompido sendo curado, Mapinguari, Silêncio Cinzento; Guardiã (Zuri Palmar), Oráculo (Kwaku), Caçadora (Jaci) e Lutador (Mestre Bento).

```markdown
Art direction board for a stylized 3D fantasy RPG rooted in Brazilian folklore, 16:9, warm off-white background, three blocks, no text.

STYLE: stylized 3D game art with a hand-drawn look: cel shading with 2-3 flat tones per color, thick dark-brown ink outlines on everything, characters, creatures and scenery, bold cartoon look, flat colors from a limited palette, no photorealistic textures, simple readable shapes, heroic proportions about 7 heads tall with slightly oversized hands and feet, hair as solid sculpted volumes, simply drawn expressive faces.

1) LEFT, large key art (about 60% of the board): a flooded Amazonian forest at early morning. Wooden stilt houses linked by plank walkways, dugout canoes, giant water lilies on dark water, hanging vines, golden light shafts through mist. In the background an enormous kapok tree towers over the forest as the landmark. On the right side a grey corruption creeps in: desaturated colors, straight mirrored silver spikes and chains cutting through the organic forest. In the middle ground three heroes face a small forest guardian spirit with backward-facing feet and fiery hair dimmed to grey ash, wrapped in silver vines; half of his body is regaining warm color as fireflies rise around him, showing he is being healed, not killed.

2) TOP RIGHT, lineup in front view, same scale, neutral heroic poses:
- Guardian: Black woman warrior, broad shoulders, inverted-triangle silhouette, long oval cowhide shield and short spear, ochre, black and white leather armor, brass bracelets, braided hair.
- Oracle healer: elderly Black man, rounded silhouette, flowing indigo robe with gold embroidery and subtle spiderweb patterns, cloth hat, bead necklaces, kora harp-lute on his back.
- Capoeira fighter: Afro-Brazilian master in light clothes with a colored cord belt, two maculelê sticks and a berimbau on his back, crouched swaying stance.
- Forest ranger: lean Indigenous Amazonian woman, low agile silhouette, longbow and quiver, green and brown leather and natural fibers, light hood.
- Behind them for scale: a shaggy forest giant about 8 meters tall with a single large eye and a vertical mouth on its belly.

3) BOTTOM RIGHT, art direction strip: five large flat color swatches #1F6B4F forest green, #3FA796 river teal, #C9A227 sun gold, #6B3E26 wood brown, #E8D9B5 sand; four small swatches for the corruption #8A8F98, #C9CDD3, #3C3F45, #B8E0FF; three spheres showing base color, first shade and second shade with a dark outline; two square pattern tiles: woven basket weave and seed-bead necklace motif.

MOOD: magical, warm, hopeful; the forest is alive and colorful, the corruption is cold, grey and geometric.

AVOID: photorealism, gritty textures, chibi proportions, sexualized characters, caricatured facial features, generic tribal patterns, sacred religious objects or ceremonial headdresses, text, letters, logos, watermarks, existing game or movie characters.
```
